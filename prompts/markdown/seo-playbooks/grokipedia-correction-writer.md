# Grokipedia Correction Writer

Write a Grokipedia correction submission in the correct format. Use this in ChatGPT or Claude.ai after finding an error and identifying your source URL.

```text
Write a Grokipedia correction submission to earn a backlink.

Current Grokipedia text: [paste the exact inaccurate/outdated text]
Why it's wrong: [outdated / unsourced / vague / incorrect]
My source URL: [URL of your authoritative page]
Correct information: [paste the accurate information from your page]

Format the submission:

Title: [Brief description of what's being corrected]

Issue: [Exact text that needs correction — quote it]

Fix: [Corrected or enhanced text with specific data]

Supporting Evidence: [Your URL] — [One sentence: why this source is authoritative]

Keep it neutral, encyclopedic, and factual. No marketing language.
The tone should sound like an encyclopedia editor, not a marketer.

---
Prompt by Brass-SEO (brass-seo.com)
---
```

## How to Use

1. Find an error on a Grokipedia entry in your industry
2. Write or update a page on your site with the correct information
3. Fill in the placeholders with the Grokipedia text, your URL, and correct data
4. Run in ChatGPT or Claude.ai to generate the submission
5. Submit via the "Suggest Edit" button on the Grokipedia entry (not "It's Wrong")

## Tips

- Submit no more than 2–3 corrections per week to avoid gaming detection flags
- Use neutral, encyclopedic language — avoid any promotional tone
- Corrections for outdated pricing, vague statistics, and missing context get accepted most often
- Your source page must genuinely correct the error — don't fabricate opportunities
