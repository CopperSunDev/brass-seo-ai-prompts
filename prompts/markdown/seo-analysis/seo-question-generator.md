# SEO Question Generator

## Overview

Generate a tailored list of SEO questions for your specific business and website. Helps users who are not sure what to ask about their SEO data get started with targeted, actionable questions.

## The Prompt

```text
Generate a list of 15-20 specific SEO questions I should ask about my website, tailored to my business type and goals.

Website: [your website URL]
Business Type: [e.g., local plumber, online store, SaaS company, restaurant, law firm, freelancer]
Main Pages: [list your 3-5 most important pages, e.g., /services, /pricing, /blog, /contact]
Current SEO Knowledge: [beginner, intermediate, or advanced]
Main Goal: [e.g., more local customers, more online sales, more blog traffic, better conversions]

Generate questions organized into these categories:

1. TRAFFIC & TRENDS (4-5 questions)
   - Questions about overall traffic direction
   - Period-over-period comparisons
   - Channel-specific traffic questions
   - Seasonal or trend-related questions

2. PAGE PERFORMANCE (3-4 questions)
   - Questions about specific important pages
   - Questions comparing pages against each other
   - Questions about engagement and user behavior on key pages

3. KEYWORD OPPORTUNITIES (3-4 questions)
   - Questions about ranking positions and potential
   - Questions about keyword gaps and new content ideas
   - Questions about competitor-adjacent keywords

4. TECHNICAL HEALTH (2-3 questions)
   - Questions about indexing status
   - Questions about crawl issues or errors
   - Questions about page speed or mobile issues

5. STRATEGIC PRIORITIES (3-4 questions)
   - Questions about where to focus effort this month
   - Questions about quick wins vs long-term projects
   - Questions about what is working vs what needs attention

Make each question specific to my business type and pages. Avoid generic questions — include my actual page URLs and business context in the questions. Write them so I can paste each one directly into an SEO analysis tool.

---
Prompt by Brass-SEO (brass-seo.com)
---
```

## How to Use

1. Replace the placeholders with your business details
2. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI tool
3. Review the generated questions and pick the most relevant ones
4. Paste each question into Brass-SEO's chat (or any SEO tool) to get answers
5. Use the answers to build your SEO action plan

## Tips

- Run this whenever you are not sure what to check next
- Update your business details and re-run it quarterly as your site evolves
- Beginners: start with the Traffic and Strategic Priorities questions first
- Pair with the SEO Report Action Plan prompt to turn answers into organized tasks
