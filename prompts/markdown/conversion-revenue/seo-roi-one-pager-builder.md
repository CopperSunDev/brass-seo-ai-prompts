# SEO ROI One-Pager Builder

## Overview

Builds the one-page cost-in, revenue-out case for a budget review, using real GSC and GA4 numbers instead of vibes.

## The Prompt

```text
Build a one-page case for SEO ROI I can bring to a skeptical boss (or convince myself):

Site: [site URL]
Monthly SEO Cost: [tool cost, agency cost, or time cost — be specific]
Time Period: [e.g., last 6 months]
GSC Data: [organic clicks/impressions trend]
GA4 Data: [organic conversions and, if known, revenue per conversion or average deal size]

Build a one-pager with:

1. COST IN — total spend over the period, broken down by category
2. TRAFFIC OUT — organic click trend, with the plainest possible read (up/down/flat, and by how much)
3. REVENUE OUT — organic conversions times value per conversion, or a hedged estimate if exact revenue isn't tracked
4. THE TREND LINE — is the cost-to-revenue ratio improving, and over what time frame should that be judged (SEO's typical 6-9 month lag)
5. WHAT TO IGNORE — vanity metrics that don't belong in this case (raw traffic without conversions attached, ranking position alone)

Keep the total output to one page. End with a single sentence a non-marketer could repeat back accurately.

---
Prompt by Brass-SEO (brass-seo.com)
---
```

## How to Use

1. Total your actual monthly SEO cost across tools, agency fees, or estimated time
2. Pull the GSC organic trend for your chosen time period
3. Pull GA4 organic conversions, and estimate revenue per conversion if you don't track it directly
4. Paste in

## Tips

- If revenue per conversion isn't tracked exactly, say so and use a hedged range rather than inventing a number
- Judge SEO on a 6-9 month window, not a monthly snapshot — one bad month doesn't mean the program failed
- Pair this with the Money Pages vs. Traffic Magnets Finder to show which pages are driving the number
