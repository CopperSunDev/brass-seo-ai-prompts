# Grokipedia Opportunity Finder

Identify which pages on your site have the authoritative, factual content needed to support a Grokipedia correction backlink. Run this in your Brass-SEO chat.

```text
Help me find Grokipedia link building opportunities using my site data.

Identify content on my site that is authoritative enough to serve as a correction source:
1. Pages with specific factual data, statistics, or original research
2. Pages with authoritative technical explanations or process documentation
3. Content that makes verifiable, specific claims (not marketing language)

For each candidate page:
- What factual claim does it contain?
- How verifiable/specific is the data?
- What kind of Grokipedia correction could this support?

What industry topics in my niche might have outdated or unsourced Grokipedia entries?

---
Prompt by Brass-SEO (brass-seo.com)
---
```

## How to Use

1. Paste this prompt into your Brass-SEO chat
2. Brass-SEO will scan your crawled pages for authoritative, factual content
3. Take the page suggestions and search Grokipedia for related entries with errors
4. Use the Grokipedia Correction Writer prompt to draft your submission

## Tips

- Pages with statistics, pricing data, or original research make the strongest correction sources
- Search Grokipedia for topics in your niche before checking your content
- Outdated software pricing and vague industry statistics are the most common correction opportunities
