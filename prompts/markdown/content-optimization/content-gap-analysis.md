# Content Gap Analysis

## Overview

Identify content opportunities by analyzing what competitors rank for that you don't. Find missing topics, underserved keywords, and content format gaps to outperform competition.

## The Prompt

```text
Perform a content gap analysis comparing my website to competitors:

My Website: [your website URL]
Competitors: [competitor 1 URL], [competitor 2 URL], [competitor 3 URL]
Industry/Niche: [your industry]
Target Audience: [describe your audience]

Analyze and identify:

1. TOPIC GAPS
   - Topics competitors cover that I don't
   - Subtopics within my existing content that need expansion
   - Trending topics in my niche I'm missing
   - Prioritize by search volume and competition level

2. KEYWORD GAPS
   - Keywords competitors rank for (positions 1-20) that I don't target
   - Long-tail variations I'm missing
   - Question-based keywords not addressed
   - Featured snippet opportunities

3. CONTENT FORMAT GAPS
   - Content types competitors use (guides, tools, videos, infographics)
   - Interactive content opportunities
   - Resource pages or link-worthy assets missing
   - User-generated content possibilities

4. CONTENT DEPTH GAPS
   - Topics where competitors have more comprehensive coverage
   - Areas where my content is thin or outdated
   - Missing supporting content (FAQs, case studies, examples)

5. INTENT GAPS
   - Search intents not served (informational, commercial, transactional)
   - Funnel stages with missing content
   - User questions left unanswered

For each gap identified, provide:
- Priority level (High/Medium/Low)
- Estimated search volume range
- Competition difficulty
- Suggested content title
- Recommended word count/format
- Key points to cover

Create a 90-day content plan addressing the top 10 gaps.

---
Prompt by Brass-SEO (brass-seo.com)
---
```

## How to Use

1. Replace `[your website URL]` with your site
2. Add 2-3 competitor URLs you want to analyze against
3. Specify your industry and target audience
4. For best results, include any keyword data you have from SEO tools

## Expected Output

- Categorized gap analysis across 5 dimensions
- Prioritized list of content opportunities
- 90-day content calendar
- Specific content briefs for top opportunities

## Tips

- Include your top 3-5 direct competitors
- Combine with SEMrush or Ahrefs gap data for accuracy
- Focus on gaps where you have expertise to create quality content
- Revisit quarterly as competitive landscape changes
