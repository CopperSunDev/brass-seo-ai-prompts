# PAA Keyword Candidates

Analyze your GSC data in Brass-SEO to identify the best keywords for a People Also Ask (PAA) FAQ post. Run this in your Brass-SEO chat to get data-driven PAA keyword targets.

```text
Analyze my GSC data and identify my best keywords for a People Also Ask (PAA) FAQ post. Looking for:

1. Keywords with decent impressions but moderate positions (8–30)
2. Informational keywords likely to show PAA boxes (how, what, why, best, guide)
3. Keywords that could anchor a 25–30 question FAQ post

Please identify:
- Top 5–10 PAA keyword candidates with current position and impressions
- Whether any existing pages already cover these (to avoid cannibalization)
- Which keyword has the strongest PAA FAQ post potential

---
Prompt by Brass-SEO (brass-seo.com)
---
```

## How to Use

1. Paste this prompt into your Brass-SEO chat
2. Brass-SEO will analyze your Google Search Console data to find candidates
3. Take the top keyword to the next step: PAA FAQ Post Writer prompt

## Tips

- Look for informational keywords with 1,000+ monthly impressions
- Position 8–30 range gives the most upside from PAA optimization
- Compare results with a manual Google search to confirm PAA boxes appear for your keyword
