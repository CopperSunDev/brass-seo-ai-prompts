# PAA FAQ Post Writer

After you've collected real People Also Ask questions from Google SERP research, use this prompt to write a comprehensive FAQ post in ChatGPT or Claude.ai.

```text
I'm creating a People Also Ask FAQ post to rank in Google's PAA boxes.

Target keyword: [from your Brass-SEO analysis]
PAA questions I collected from Google: [paste 25–30 real questions from SERP research]
My existing posts to reference: [list any relevant posts to link from answers]

Write a complete FAQ post:
- Title: "[Topic] Questions Answered: [N]+ Expert Solutions"
- 2-paragraph intro establishing topical authority
- Quick navigation section with anchor links
- Each question as an H2 heading (exact PAA wording from Google)
- 150–300 word answer per question
- Internal links where appropriate
- FAQ schema JSON-LD at the end

Use the exact question wording from Google PAA boxes — do not rewrite them.

---
Prompt by Brass-SEO (brass-seo.com)
---
```

## How to Use

1. First, run the PAA Keyword Candidates prompt in Brass-SEO to identify your target keyword
2. Search that keyword in Google (incognito) and record 25–30 PAA questions by clicking through cascades
3. Paste those real questions into this prompt along with your existing posts
4. Run in ChatGPT, Claude.ai, or any AI tool

## Tips

- Collect questions manually from Google — never let the AI guess what they are
- Click each PAA question to reveal 4 more (cascade effect)
- Aim for 25–30 unique questions per post for maximum PAA coverage
- Target posts 10,000+ words for strongest topical authority signal
