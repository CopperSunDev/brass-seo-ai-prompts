# GA4 Traffic Analysis

## Overview

Analyze your Google Analytics 4 traffic data to understand performance trends, identify engagement issues, and get actionable SEO recommendations. Paste your GA4 data and get a structured analysis covering traffic health, engagement quality, opportunities, and a prioritized action plan.

## The Prompt

```text
Analyze the following GA4 traffic data for my website and provide actionable SEO recommendations:

Website: [your website URL]
Time Period: [e.g., last 30 days, last quarter]
Industry: [your industry]

GA4 Data (paste from your reports or describe):
- Total Sessions: [number]
- Organic Search Sessions: [number]
- Engagement Rate: [percentage]
- Bounce Rate: [percentage]
- Top Landing Pages: [list your top 5-10 landing pages with their sessions and engagement rates]
- Traffic Channels Breakdown: [organic, direct, referral, social percentages]

Please analyze:

1. TRAFFIC HEALTH
   - Is organic search growing, flat, or declining compared to previous period?
   - What percentage of total traffic comes from organic search?
   - Are there any concerning trends in the data?

2. ENGAGEMENT QUALITY
   - Which landing pages have the best engagement rates?
   - Which pages have high traffic but poor engagement (potential content-intent mismatch)?
   - What does the bounce rate suggest about content quality?

3. OPPORTUNITIES
   - Which pages should be prioritized for SEO improvement?
   - Are there pages with good engagement but low traffic (ranking opportunity)?
   - What traffic channels are underperforming?

4. ACTION PLAN
   - Top 3 immediate fixes based on the data
   - Top 3 medium-term optimizations (next 30 days)
   - Metrics to track for measuring improvement

Prioritize recommendations by expected impact on organic traffic.

---
Prompt by Brass-SEO (brass-seo.com)
---
```

## How to Use

1. Open GA4 and navigate to **Reports > Acquisition > Traffic Acquisition** for channel data
2. Go to **Reports > Engagement > Landing Page** for page-level data
3. Copy the relevant numbers into the placeholder fields
4. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI tool for analysis

## Expected Output

- Traffic health assessment with trend analysis
- Engagement quality breakdown by landing page
- Prioritized list of SEO improvement opportunities
- 30-day action plan with specific, measurable recommendations

## Tips

- Include data from two time periods (current vs. previous) for trend analysis
- Add your Google Search Console data alongside GA4 for a more complete picture
- Mention your business goals so the AI can prioritize recommendations accordingly
- Run this analysis monthly to track progress
