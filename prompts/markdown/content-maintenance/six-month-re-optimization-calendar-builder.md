# 6-Month Re-Optimization Calendar Builder

## Overview

Builds a recurring refresh calendar for a site's top pages so winners get revisited before they quietly decline.

## The Prompt

```text
Build a recurring re-optimization calendar for my site's top pages so winners don't quietly decline:

Site: [site URL]
Top Pages: [list your top 10-20 pages by traffic or revenue]
Publish/Last-Updated Dates: [for each page, if known]
Team Capacity: [how many refreshes per month realistically]

Build a 6-month calendar that:
1. Schedules each top page for a check-in at least once every 6 months
2. Front-loads pages that are already showing early decay signals (if provided) or are furthest past their last update
3. Spaces refreshes evenly against stated team capacity — don't front-load month 1 and leave months 4-6 empty
4. Distinguishes a QUICK CHECK (verify stats/dates still accurate, 15 minutes) from a FULL REFRESH (rewrite outdated sections, 1-2 hours)

Output as a month-by-month table: month, page, check type, and the one thing most likely to need updating on that page.

---
Prompt by Brass-SEO (brass-seo.com)
---
```

## How to Use

1. List your top 10-20 pages by traffic or revenue
2. Note the last-updated date for each, if known
3. State how many refreshes your team can realistically do per month
4. Paste in

## Tips

- A calendar that's never followed is worthless — set capacity honestly, not aspirationally
- Feed pages flagged by the Content Decay Detector to the front of the schedule
- Revisit the calendar itself every 6 months as the top-pages list changes
