# DIY SEO Audit Checklist

## Overview

Generate a comprehensive, step-by-step SEO audit checklist tailored to your website. Covers technical health, on-page SEO, content quality, search performance, user behavior, and prioritized action items — all using free Google tools.

## The Prompt

```text
Create a step-by-step SEO audit checklist for my website that I can complete using only free tools (Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, and manual page review).

Website: [your website URL]
CMS/Platform: [e.g., WordPress, Shopify, Squarespace, Next.js, custom]
Business Type: [e.g., local service, e-commerce, SaaS, blog, professional services]
Number of Pages: [approximate, e.g., 10-20, 50-100, 500+]
Main Goal: [e.g., more organic traffic, better conversions, local visibility]

Please generate a checklist covering:

1. TECHNICAL HEALTH
   - Indexing status (how to check in GSC)
   - Crawl errors and excluded pages
   - Mobile usability issues
   - Page speed (using PageSpeed Insights)
   - Sitemap status
   - robots.txt review

2. ON-PAGE SEO (check for top 10 pages)
   - Title tag (present, unique, descriptive, under 60 characters)
   - Meta description (present, compelling, under 160 characters)
   - H1 heading (one per page, includes target keyword)
   - Heading hierarchy (H2, H3 used logically)
   - Internal links (each page links to and from other relevant pages)
   - Image alt text (descriptive, not keyword-stuffed)

3. CONTENT QUALITY
   - Does each page serve a clear search intent?
   - Is the content substantially different from other pages on the site?
   - Are there thin pages that should be expanded or consolidated?
   - Is the information accurate and up to date?

4. SEARCH PERFORMANCE (from GSC data)
   - Top pages by clicks (are these the right pages?)
   - Pages with high impressions but low CTR (title/description issues?)
   - Keywords in positions 5-20 (ranking opportunities)
   - Pages with declining clicks (need attention?)

5. USER BEHAVIOR (from GA4 data)
   - Top landing pages by sessions
   - Pages with low engagement rates (content-intent mismatch?)
   - Bounce rate by page (compared to site average)
   - Conversion paths (which pages lead to goals?)

6. PRIORITY ACTION PLAN
   - Rank findings by expected impact (high, medium, low)
   - List specific fixes for the top 5 issues
   - Estimate effort for each fix (quick win vs. larger project)
   - Suggest a timeline for completing fixes

Tailor the checklist to my specific platform and business type. Flag any checks that are especially important for my situation.

---
Prompt by Brass-SEO (brass-seo.com)
---
```

## How to Use

1. Replace the placeholders with your website details
2. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI tool
3. Work through the generated checklist systematically
4. Use GSC and GA4 to complete each check
5. Focus on the Priority Action Plan at the end

## Tips

- Run this audit quarterly for the best results
- Have your GSC and GA4 dashboards open while working through the checklist
